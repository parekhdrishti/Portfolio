PROJECT_03
F1 Qualifying Performance & Race Outcomes
Data analysis
2025

A statistical study of 7,134 Formula 1 race entries (2000–2024) testing how strongly qualifying position predicts race outcome. Found a Spearman correlation of 0.785, an average finish within 3.06 places of the grid slot, and podium rates of 73.93% for Top 3 starters versus 1.86% for P11+. Built an interactive Streamlit dashboard for exploring trends by team, driver, and era.
